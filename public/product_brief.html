Seeking Alpha Legal turns the published record of the Singapore International Commercial Court and the Singapore Supreme Court into a searchable intelligence layer over judges, law firms, and individual counsel. It reads primary-source Grounds of Decision so litigators and firm leaders can find parallel cases, benchmark counsel by sector, and study the court's accepted reasoning before they file, brief, or hire.

Why it is different
Singapore-native. Global platforms such as Lex Machina, Lexis, and Westlaw are built on United States dockets and do not cover Singapore. This is built on the Singapore record.
Primary-source. Every figure traces to a published Grounds of Decision on eLitigation, not a third-party summary.
Beyond win rates. Records are stratified by sector and issue to surface real streaks, with the winning arguments extracted, not just a headline percentage.
Talent-aware. The only view that maps the individual counsel behind the wins, for scouting and lateral strategy.
Methodology & delivery
How it is built

Judgments are parsed from public eLitigation Grounds of Decision. Outcomes are read from the disposition and case summary, counsel and firms from the judgment header, and reasoning from a framing engine that tags interpretive registers, authorities, and accepted arguments. Figures are descriptive and rest on the cited record.

Access

Web dashboard with a quarterly data refresh. API access and bespoke investigative research are available through Seeking Alpha Advisors.

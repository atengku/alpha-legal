The qualitative layer of litigation

Harvey automates the paperwork. Charlie Legal decodes the verdict.

The legal AI race is a race to do the work faster. We are not in it. Charlie Legal is the transparent intelligence layer that instruments what SICC litigators have always read by instinct: how a tribunal reasons, which counsel actually wins where, and where the unspoken edge sits. Built on public record, grounded in published judgments, engineered to be defensible.

03 · Provocative, and inside the line

Judicial analytics is legitimate use of public data almost everywhere. France is the one jurisdiction that criminalised profiling named judges, up to five years in prison, precisely because firms began modelling individual judges. Singapore has no such ban, judgments are public, and the publicly-available-data position under the PDPA applies. Charlie Legal is engineered to sit on the right side of that line by design.

✓ What we do

  • Infer disposition only from a judge's own published judgments and reasoning.
  • Cite every inference to the ruling it came from, neutral and non-evaluative.
  • Rank counsel on their public litigation track record, as any market ranks performance.
  • Keep the methodology transparent and auditable end to end.

✗ The line we do not cross

  • No scrape of a judge's private or social footprint. That is the French outcome and a PDPA and contempt risk.
  • No claim to predict a named verdict, nothing that could read as scandalising the court.
  • No black-box assertion a user cannot trace to source.
  • The boundary is a design constraint that makes the asset investable, not a limitation.

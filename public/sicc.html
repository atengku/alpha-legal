How this was built, and what it is not

Source. eLitigation's SG Courts Judgments search, filtered to the SICC universe. Every judgment was captured three ways: the authoritative PDF, clean extracted text for parsing, and the raw HTML for layout fidelity.

Judges. Coram lines name the same judge several ways. “Sir Bernard Rix” and “Bernard Rix” are one person; so are “Beverley Marian McLachlin”, “Beverley McLachlin” and “Beverley McLachlin PC”. Variants are merged by surname plus a shared given name, after stripping honorific prefixes and post-nominals, so counts are per judge and not per spelling.

Awards. Only the operative court award counts. In arbitration matters “the Award” usually means the arbitral award, not money the court ordered, so arbitral context is detected and excluded. An appeal affirming an award below is the same money, not a second award. Currencies are never converted or summed.

Counsel and firms. Extracted from the counsel lines by pattern. The extractor also captured personal names and phrases such as “instructed counsel” as firms; those are filtered out here, but the underlying sweep is recall-oriented.

Treat entries as candidates, not a citator. Authorities and statutes are regex sweeps over judgment bodies. They are good for finding what to read next. They are not a verified citation index, and nothing here is legal advice.

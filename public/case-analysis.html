Extraction thesis

A defendant's win. The transferable edge is threefold: a black-letter test map with the burden calibrated by worked example; a limb-by-limb autopsy of which evidence failed; and a disposition read on Chua Lee Ming JC. The doctrinal prize is the import of the stringent Bouvier "real risk" standard into search orders [31]–[32].

Verdict on reuse: necessary, not sufficient — pair with the sourcing manifest to reach trial-ready.

Doctrinal prize · [31]–[32]

"An allegation of dishonesty is no substitute for examining whether there is in fact a real risk." Surreptitious conduct ≠ propensity to destroy. The Bouvier stringency now governs limb (c).

Limb (a) — no "extremely strong" case FAILED

  • Suspicious Transactions rebutted with supporting documents (rent, salaries, furniture, directors' fees, consultancy); plaintiff had "bare assertion" [21].
  • The Offer = legitimate without-prejudice settlement; pleadings struck out; PSW asked for "more time" — "an odd response" for an alleged bribe [22].
  • Ukraine Deal a bare allegation; corroborating recording was an inaudible transcript the court refused to rely on [23],[25].

Limb (c) — no real risk FAILED

  • Sub-S$10k transfers & signatory removal answered by cash-flow explanation "consistent with the evidence" [34].
  • Crypto "untraceable dissipation" theory = "conjecture and suspicion" [36]–[37].
  • Own-goal: PSW failed to attend a scheduled expenses review [35].

Limb (d) + framing FAILED / DEFECT

  • Order threatened investor confidence in a company needing funding [39].
  • Defendants consented to surrender laptops / clone phones — preservation rationale self-neutralised [12].
  • Remedy mismatch: Estates General buy-out "no place" in a s216 claim [28].

Bench — Chua Lee Ming JC

  • Weights proportionality heavily — can outweigh a met damage limb [39].
  • Credits documentary rebuttal over assertion [21],[34].
  • Names speculation/conjecture and discounts it [36]–[37].
  • Pattern-matches on conduct inconsistent with the narrative [22],[35].
  • Will recharacterise a mis-framed claim/remedy [28].

Acting for applicant (plaintiff)
  • Build limb (a) on corroborated documents before filing [21].
  • For (c) get evidence of actual deletion, not character [32],[37].
  • Full-and-frank disclosure checklist — omission is fatal [15].
  • Align cause of action to remedy; confirm standing [28].
  • Authenticate recordings before relying on them [25].
Acting for respondent (defendant)
  • Attack each limb independently — one suffices [17].
  • Offer voluntary preservation early to gut proportionality [12],[39].
  • Hunt the ex parte affidavit for non-disclosure [15].
  • Deploy subsequent-events + Bouvier "real risk" [16],[32].
  • Answer transactions with documents; strike collateral allegations [21],[22].
